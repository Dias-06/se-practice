# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

6 to 8 stories. Keep the shape exactly: ID, the As/I want/so that sentence, a priority, one
assumption. Roles are **Student** or **Administrator** only.

---
# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

---

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view available study rooms, so that I can choose a free room for studying.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Room availability status is accurately updated in real time.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to book a free study room for a selected time slot, so that I can reserve a space for individual or group study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** A booking cannot exceed two hours and must start in the future.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel my active room booking, so that the room becomes available for others when I no longer need it.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Students can only cancel their own future bookings.

### US-04
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a study room, so that students cannot reserve rooms under service or emergency updates.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Blocked rooms immediately become unavailable for new student bookings.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a period, so that I can analyze how study rooms are being utilized.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Historical usage data is retained and accessible for library staff.

### US-06
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive a confirmation after making or canceling a booking, so that I have proof of my reservation status.
**Priority:** Low
**Assumption:** Confirmations are generated automatically upon successful booking or cancellation actions.

### US-07
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to view all active bookings across rooms, so that I can monitor study room activity.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** Administrators have access to all room reservation schedules.
